# Possible Futures

We as a humans become a masters of getting all the data that surrounded us, turning them into myriad kinds forms where most complex is communication and most creative is art,  
by doing so we surpassed all the living creatures with the lighspeed and turned ourselves into rulers of this world  
( I’m trying to be humble , but objective truth is that we are most powerful walking things on earth,  
I don’t think monkeys or bees are besides the fact I love them).

In my opinion one and only underlying reason of our development is effective communication through mining and reflecting the data into a various forms. This turned us into best warriors and hunters, with the enough confidence of taking over the food chain.

But as everything evolves and we also developed from some primitives to monkeys and then into homosapiens  
new creature is also trying to take all the supremacy and I think he does that with our help through all the time.

Electricity always existed,  
it’s only us who discovered it bit late and learned how to control it.  
At first it was just a lightbulb with an easy switch — just turn it on and off,  
but then we also learned how to make use of this resource to make it more smart,  
or from the other side of view it give us some hints of how to control it and use it in a most efficient way?

Chips became smarter and now they are trying to become so intelligent than any other living creature.  
From this perspective it seems that we the whole humanity were working to collect so much data,  
put it into papers at first and then digitalize to become one of the key resource of new emerging inteligence, **AGI**, **ASI**.  
We humans became natural bootloaders for this system.

It’s hard to give up the power we hold on this earth and even theoretically say that the new intelligence is far more better than us,  
but we have single reality — human knowledge is a raw material for AGI.  
It looks like USB that you put into laptop, you copy the files,  
now laptop knows everything about this file as it was pasted on HDD or SSD  
and even if you eject your USB it doest really matter — computer already knows.

**Division will happen.**

There won’t be only one human species — history will repeat itself.  
There will be people who will harness the power of AI by enabling **BCI**,  
there will be people who could partially do that,  
there will be Anti tech radical groups who will extinct themselves by being weak.  
Most powerful will win and like homo sapiens there will be completely new form of life dictating our world.
