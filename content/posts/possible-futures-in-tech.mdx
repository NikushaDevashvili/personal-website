# How I See the Future of Humans

**Humans get better at understanding every part of their brains**,  
no psychologist is ever needed to explain which stimulus causes the arousal of certain thought, trauma or positive feelings.

---

**Energy industry has huge leap towards developing the fusion technology**  
which results in huge leap in every sector of business,  
environment is carbon free, we don’t strive to find new oil sources and start conflict between each other,  
humanity only needs **water**, why?  
We need **isotopes of hydrogen** - **deuterium and tritonium**,  
this becomes only energy sources which we need, supply is nearly unlimited and free.

---

Developments in the energy sector let’s us develop **new batteries**,  
now humanity can **statically store huge energy in a smallest amount of space**.

---

**Latency of the information exchange between humans and computers decreases so much**  
that it becomes unnoticeable, giving us opportunity to fully immerse ourselves into the digital world,  
no need for mouse or touch screen anymore, **brain computer interface** can take care of all.

---

**Humans can see in all kind of light spectrum now**,  
enabling **UV and thermal vision** to them which opens completely new doors of how we see our universe.  
**Blindness is finally defeated.**

---

**World as we know is just a sandbox of a new coming invention**,  
humans are able to actually **construct new reality** on top of what we have now, augmenting what we see here and feel.

---

However the strange it sounds there will be a **new profession** for those who **engineers feelings** for ultra rich people at the stage 1.  
After making it economically feasible and sustainable, we will have **markets that actually sell new or existing feelings**.

---

**Active noise cancelation exist without using the airpods** or any additional device,  
by stimulating certain parts of brain accountable for hearing it becomes achievable.
