What makes your soul move? What makes you feel alive?  
What makes you truly understand the principle of relativity — so much that doing something special warps the time, and it passes as you can’t even notice?

Those things to work on are hard to find,  
but every human being has in itself the calling to something —  
something that is hidden and buried in ourselves,  
that it takes so much effort and patience to hear it whisper.

And sometimes when we hear it, we neglect it immediately,  
because the power and talent it gives us is scary —  
not only for us but also for the society.  
And even if we convince ourselves to do it, society will push us  
and convince it’s trash and brings nothing good to us.

Those things doesn’t bring immediate monetary value,  
so it’s hard for our underdeveloped will to see its beauty.  
As true investors invest in the long term,  
we must receive this gift as a present that uncovers itself in the long run.

If you need pomodoro you are in a wrong place,  
if you need tablets to concentrate you concentrate on a wrong matter,  
if you convince yourself that your work brings lot’s of monetary incentives and it worth something you work in a wrong place.  
If your job brings you a lot of artificial stress but no relief you are stressing in a wrong place.

Then you need coffee, nicotine, brain optimizer, enhancers,  
because you did so much damage to yourself that it can’t express its genius.

Find something that truly brings a joy, and you will see you won’t need pomodoro to concentrate.  
Try something you can give your heart to, and you won’t notice how time passes — now time works in favor.

Don’t be afraid of your genius.  
Give yourself a space and let out **boogie wooogie.**
