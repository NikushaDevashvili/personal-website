## Understanding "Teeing"

Teeing - River (the Data Stream) flowing to a Village (the User).

- **The Problem:** You (Raindrop) want to test the water quality, but you cannot stop the river or the village will dry up.
- **The Physics:** You cannot "read" the water twice. Once it flows past you, it's gone.
- **The Solution:** You dig a small Canal sideways. You divert 1% of the water (or copy it) into a testing pool. The main river keeps flowing to the village at full speed.

In Computer Science, this is called **Teeing** (named after a 'T' shaped pipe).

Long before linux sysadmins had this problem, they wanted to see logs on the screen and at the same time save this log into log.txt file. So they were using Teeing method

Echo would send the data
Tee splitter it into two parts - Path A: would go into the screen so that user sees (“hello world”), Path B would go to log.txt (file would save log in this case: “hello world”)

```
echo “hello word” | tee log.txt
```

In our case it looks like this:

```
const [userStream, trackerStream] = originalStream.tee();
```

- **userStream:** You give this back to the app immediately. The app consumes it at full speed.
- **raindropStream:** You keep this. You read it slowly in the background to analyze hallucinations.
